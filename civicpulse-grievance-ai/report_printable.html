1. Executive Summary & Problem Formulation

Municipal administrative bodies receive thousands of complaints daily concerning critical public infrastructure, utility breakdowns, civic hazards, and public safety issues. Citizens report these grievances using informal, unstructured conversational language in diverse formats including English, Hindi, and code-mixed romanized Hinglish.

Existing municipal redressal setups rely on manual clerical screening. Human dispatchers must inspect each complaint, assign a department, guess its severity, and forward it to field engineers. This manual process causes catastrophic dispatch delays for life-threatening emergencies (e.g., live electric wires, stalking, missing children), widespread misrouting between overlapping departments, and duplicate ticket storms when dozens of citizens report the same incident.

CivicPulse AI resolves this urban governance bottleneck by integrating high-speed multi-task Machine Learning with a domain-adapted Hinglish normalizer, Civic Named Entity Recognition, life-safety priority heuristic overrides, incident spike clustering, Explainable AI (XAI), and dual municipal work order dispatching.

4. Machine Learning & Natural Language Processing Architecture

4.1 Code-Mixed Hinglish Normalization

The normalizer conducts a two-stage transformation. First, multi-word idiom regex patterns are replaced with canonical semantic expressions (e.g., "paani nahi aa raha" → "total water supply outage no water"). Second, token-level romanized Hindi terms (e.g., bijli, gaddha, kooda, machhar, rishwat, andhera) are transliterated. The code-mix ratio determines whether the complaint is pure English, English with vernacular terms, or code-mixed Hinglish.

4.2 Civic Named Entity Recognition (Civic-NER)

Rule-based entity extraction recognizes municipal jurisdictions across 8 urban wards (Ward 01 to Ward 08), physical infrastructure asset tags (e.g., Pole #12, Transformer TX-4, Manhole #8), educational landmarks (DAV Public School, Bal Bharti School), and duration markers (3 days, kal se).

4.3 Multi-Task Department & Subcategory Classifier

Combines TfidfVectorizer(ngram_range=(1, 2)) with calibrated LogisticRegression(C=2.0) for department classification, and MultinomialNB(alpha=0.1) for granular subcategory identification.

4.4 Dynamic Urgency Scoring & Heuristic Escalation

Base risk scores (40) are adjusted dynamically based on severity indicators. Critical emergency keywords (e.g., electrocution, live wire, missing child, stalking, flooding, bribe) trigger immediate deterministic overrides to P1 (Critical Emergency), ensuring life-safety risks bypass normal queues.

4.5 Explainable AI (XAI) & Incident Deduplication

Feature attributions highlight exact trigger words (Red = Emergency, Amber = Severity, Purple = Distress) alongside a distress score (-1.0 to +1.0). In parallel, an unsupervised TF-IDF Cosine Similarity engine checks active grievances within the same ward and department; complaints exceeding a 0.30 similarity threshold are merged into a Master Cluster, tracking affected citizens and triggering surge alerts when clusters reach 3+ or 5+ reports.

6. REST API Architecture

Method Endpoint Functionality
GET / Serves Citizen Grievance Portal (HTML/JS/CSS).
GET /admin Serves Municipal Officer Command Center.
POST /api/grievance/analyze Real-time live AI triage returning Department, Priority, SLA, Ward, and XAI tokens.
POST /api/grievance/submit Ingests complaint, performs deduplication, generates Work Order and SMS notifications.
GET /api/grievance/track/{id} Tracks ticket milestone progress, department status, and SLA deadlines.
GET /api/admin/tickets Returns triage queues filtered by department, priority, ward, or status.
POST /api/admin/tickets/{id}/action Executes officer triage actions (Escalate, In-Progress, Resolve).
GET /api/admin/clusters Returns active deduplication incident clusters and citizen counts.
GET /api/admin/analytics Returns aggregated SLA compliance and department workload statistics.
GET/POST /api/admin/batch-benchmark Executes live NLP model evaluation across benchmark grievances.
